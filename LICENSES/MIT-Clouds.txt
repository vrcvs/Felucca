The CLOUDS effect's DSP is ported from Emilie Gillet's eurorack code
<https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack> (MIT):

- src/clouds_dsp.c: Clouds' granular processor (clouds/dsp/granular_processor.cc, grain.h, window.h,
  correlator.cc, wsola_sample_player.h, audio_buffer.h, looping_sample_player.h, frame.h, fx/diffuser.h,
  fx/pitch_shifter.h, fx/reverb.h, mu_law.cc, pvoc/*: phase_vocoder.cc, stft.cc, frame_transformation.cc) and the
  stmlib parts it uses (SoftLimit, the SRC filters, fast_atan2r), in fixed point;
- tools/gen_clouds_tables.py: its tables, from clouds/resources/lookup_tables.py and src_filters.py.

Ported to fixed-point C for Felucca, 2026. The ported files stay under the MIT licence below; the glue that puts
the effect in the mix (src/fx_clouds.c) and the rest of Felucca is GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE).
